Privacy & local storage

DocuScan Pro is local-first. Your documents are processed in your browser and stored in IndexedDB on your device. Documents are not uploaded to any server. OCR runs entirely on-device when the language assets are cached; otherwise the OCR engine downloads them on first use.

Browser-local storage is not cloud backup. If you clear site data, reset the browser, or remove storage, saved documents will be lost.
