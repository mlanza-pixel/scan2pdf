Your illustrated quick-start

From paper to PDF.
In a few taps.

A picture-first guide to scanning, cropping, straightening, reading text and signing PDFs on your iPad. Pick the job you want to do below.

Choose your mission

Mission 01 · Scan a document

📷 Make a searchable PDF

  1. Tap “Choose Photo or Camera”. On iPad, select Take Photo or choose a picture from Photos/Files.
  2. Check the preview. If Auto straighten is on, the app attempts to find the page and square it up. If it misses, use Crop last page and drag the blue corners around the paper.
  3. Improve readability. Try Enhance last page or select Black & white document for OCR. Use Restore if the result looks worse.
  4. Tap “Recognize & Create PDF”. Wait for processing, then check the OCR preview for mistakes.
  5. Download PDF or share it. Use TXT/DOCX when you want editable words.
Mission 02 · Existing file

✍️ Sign a PDF you already have

  1. Tap “Open PDF from Files”. Pick an existing PDF on your iPad.
  2. Select the page number where your signature belongs.
  3. Choose a position (such as bottom right). Drag the signature to the correct place on the page.
  4. Draw your signature with your finger or Apple Pencil, or type your name. Tap Clear signature to try again.
  5. Tap “Apply signature & save”, then Download signed PDF or Share signed PDF. Open the result and check its placement.
⭐ Best OCR tip: Fill the photo with the page, avoid shadows and reflections, keep the camera parallel to the document, and crop away the table or computer-screen borders before recognizing text. OCR is not perfect—always compare important text with the original.

What do these buttons do?

✂️CropKeep only the page. Drag the four blue corner handles; tap Apply crop.
▱Auto straightenTries to detect edges and correct perspective. May fail on cluttered backgrounds.
↻RotateTurn the last scanned page 90° when it is sideways.
◐EnhanceIncrease contrast for faint print. Restore if fine detail disappears.
＋Add pageMake a multipage PDF. Repeat for each sheet, then run OCR.
↗Share / ExportPDF keeps the photographed page; TXT/DOC/DOCX contain editable OCR text.

If something goes wrong

Camera shows black

Use Choose Photo or Camera and pick a photo from the iPad Photos app instead. Check Safari camera permission if you want to capture directly.

Text looks wrong

Crop tighter, use better light, try another OCR mode, then retake. Correct mistakes in the editable text box. The PDF's hidden OCR layer may still need review.

Mission 03 · PDF Tools

🧰 Edit a PDF you already have

  1. Open PDF from Files to select your document.
  2. Scroll to PDF Tools and select a tool: merge, split/extract, reorder, rotate, delete, watermark, number, flatten, extract text, JPG or compress.
  3. Enter page numbers when relevant. For example, 1,3-5 selects pages 1, 3, 4 and 5.
  4. Tap Run selected tool, then Download or Share result.
  5. Tap Use result for next tool to make another edit or sign the result.

For signing, use the separate signing panel. These tools are experimental until tested on your iPad.
